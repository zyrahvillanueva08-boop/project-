What is Biotechnology?

Biotechnology is a field of technology that uses living organisms, cells, or biological processes to develop useful products and solutions. It is commonly used in areas such as healthcare, agriculture, food production, and environmental protection.

Purpose

The purpose of biotechnology is to use biological processes to create useful products, improve existing processes, and solve different problems. It can help develop medicines, improve crops, and support healthier and more sustainable ways of producing resources.

Goal

The goal of biotechnology is to use science and living organisms to improve people's lives and the environment. It aims to develop safer, more effective, and sustainable solutions for different needs.
