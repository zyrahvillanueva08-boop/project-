What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is technology that allows computers and machine to learn, understand information, and perform task that normally require human intelligence.

Purpose

The purpose of Artificial Intelligence is to help people complete tasks faster and easier. It can process information, solve problems, and assist people in making decisions. AI is designed to make work more efficient and useful in different areas.

Goal

The goal of Artificial Intelligence is to create smart systems that can learn, understand information, and solve problems. It aims to support people in their daily activities, improve productivity, and provide useful solutions in different fields.
