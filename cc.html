What is Cloud Computing?

Cloud computing is a technology that allows people to store, access, and manage data and applications through the internet instead of relying only on a computer or physical storage device. It allows users to access their files and services from different devices as long as they have an internet connection.

Purpose

The purpose of cloud computing is to provide convenient and flexible access to data, applications, and online services. It helps people and organizations store information, share files, and use computing resources without needing large amounts of physical storage.

Goal

The goal of cloud computing is to make data and digital services easier to access, manage, and share. It aims to provide reliable, flexible, and efficient technology that can support users, businesses, schools, and other organizations.
