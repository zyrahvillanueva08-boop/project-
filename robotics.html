What is Robotics?

Robotics is a field of technology that involves designing, building, and programming robots that can perform different tasks. Robots can be controlled by people or programmed to work automatically. They are used in areas such as manufacturing, healthcare, education, exploration, and everyday activities.

Purpose

The purpose of robotics is to help people perform tasks that may be difficult, repetitive, or require high precision. Robots can assist humans, improve productivity, and make certain tasks faster and more efficient.

Goal

The goal of robotics is to develop useful and reliable robots that can safely assist people and perform tasks effectively. It aims to improve work processes, support different industries, and create solutions for real-world problems.
