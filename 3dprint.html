What is 3D printing?

3D printing is a technology that creates physical objects by building them layer by layer from a digital design. It uses materials such as plastic, resin, or other suitable materials to produce different shapes and objects. It is commonly used in education, manufacturing, design, engineering, and healthcare.

Purpose

The purpose of 3D printing is to create customized and useful objects quickly and efficiently. It can help turn digital designs into physical models, prototypes, tools, and other products.

Goal

The goal of 3D printing is to make the process of designing and producing objects easier, faster, and more flexible. It aims to reduce material waste and allow people to create detailed and customized products.
