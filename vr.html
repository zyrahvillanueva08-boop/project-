What is Virtual Reality?

Virtual Reality is a technology that creates a computer-generated environment where users can see, hear, and interact with a simulated world. It uses devices such as VR headsets and controllers to make the experience feel more realistic and immersive. VR can be used for education, training, entertainment, gaming, and exploring different environments.

Purpose

The purpose of VR is to provide an interactive and realistic experience that can be used for learning, training, entertainment, and exploring different environments.

Goal

The goal of VR is to make digital experiences more immersive and engaging. It aims to help people learn, practice skills, and experience situations in a safe and controlled environment.
